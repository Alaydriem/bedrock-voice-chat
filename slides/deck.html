Bedrock Voice Chat

A free Bedrock and Java addon that lets Bedrock players experience proximity voice and group chats with their friends.

What makes up BVC?

BVC is a system made up of three separate components

  1. 1 The Voice Chat Server Which this video is covering. Server
  2. 2 The Bedrock Dedicated Server Addon Or the Realms and Aternos Addon (or Java mod/plugin) World
  3. 3 The client app PC, Mac, Linux, Android, or iPhone — and on console using the mobile app. Player

Getting started

What you'll need

  1. 1 A domain name .stream domains are under $5 USD for both the initial year and the yearly renewal. I recommend Cloudflare as your registrar, your DNS manager, and for free TLS. Under $5 USD / yr
  2. 2 A hosting service Such as Digital Ocean. For servers under 5 players, BVC runs on a $4 droplet. From $4 / mo

Hosting

BVC runs in a variety of different places

  • On your home network On a computer with port forwarding.
  • On an existing Java server Where you have Simple Voice Chat installed.
  • On several Minecraft hosts Such as Nodecraft, when running the Java BVC mod on a Geyser server.
  • Anywhere you can run a Docker container Vultr, DigitalOcean, that raspberry pi you forgot about

All the supported configurations are over on the Wiki at bedrockvoicechat.com/wiki.

Installing the Addon

Incredibly straightforward

  1. 1 Download the file curseforge.com/minecraft-bedrock/addons/bedrock-voice-chat
  2. 2 Rename .mcaddon to .zip and extract it
  3. 3 Upload both .mcpack files to your server bedrock-voice-chat-bp.mcpack to development_behavior_packs, -rp.mcpack to development_resource_packs

Creating a brand new world from scratch? Import the .mcaddon into Minecraft, create the world with Beta APIs enabled, and export it to your host instead.

Activate the Addon

For ease of debugging, I recommend enabling content-log-console-output-enabled=true in your server.properties before you start your world.

Logging In

The client app

And with that, you're setup!

  1. 1 Download the desktop or mobile app On console, use the mobile app.
  2. 2 Enter your Bedrock Voice Chat server address At the login screen.
  3. 3 Sign in with your Microsoft account The one associated to your Xbox Live Gamertag.

A note on your credentials

Your data only connects
to your server.

Your credentials are used exclusively to authenticate your Xbox Live Gamertag. It is not logged, or tracked, or captured. All the source code is publicly available over on GitHub.
