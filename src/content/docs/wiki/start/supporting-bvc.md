BVC started as a passion project and grew into a platform. Months of development, testing, studio audio gear, mobile test devices, and app store fees, toward one goal: real-time voice communication that is simple, reliable, and free for every Bedrock player, across platforms.

It stays free. Support pays for keeping it that way.

## Financially

**[Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/c/Alaydriem)** — funds ongoing development, app store maintenance, and new features.

**[YouTube](https://youtube.com/@alaydriem)** or **[Twitch](https://twitch.tv/alaydriem)** memberships — exclusive updates, early feature previews, and a view of development as it happens.

Supporters get **early access to builds and packs**. That matters most around Minecraft updates: on Realms and Aternos, supporters typically have a working build before general release. See [version support](/wiki/platforms/version-support/) for why that gap exists.

## For free

Not everyone can contribute financially, and these genuinely help.

**Watch and comment on [YouTube](https://youtube.com/@alaydriem).** Ad revenue is small but real, and all major BVC announcements go there first.

**Follow the [Twitch](https://www.twitch.tv/alaydriem) streams.** Twice weekly on a regular schedule. Revenue goes back into videos and development.

**Tell people about BVC, and send feedback.** Bug reports and feature requests on GitHub or Discord shape what gets built.

**Answer questions.** On [Discord](https://discord.gg/CdtchD5zxr) and [GitHub](https://github.com/Alaydriem/bedrock-voice-chat/discussions). Someone who knows the answer saving someone else an afternoon is the most valuable thing in this list.

## Where it goes

Server costs, client improvements, platform releases, and the reverse-engineering work that keeps Realms and Aternos working across Minecraft updates.
