Bedrock Voice Chat gives creators multi-track recording, Stream Deck control, and a local API for your own tools. Each player records to a separate track. You can fix the audio of one player in post-production and not change the other tracks.

| Page | Contents |
|---|---|
| [Recording](/wiki/creator/recording/) | Record a session and export a track for each player as BWAV or MP4/Opus. |
| [Stream Deck](/wiki/creator/stream-deck/) | Mute, deafen and record from an Elgato Stream Deck. |
| [OBS Overlay](/wiki/creator/obs-overlay/) | Show who you can hear, and who is talking, in OBS. |
| [WebSocket API](/wiki/creator/websocket-api/) | Use the same controls from your own tools. |

:::note
Recording is available only on desktop. Mobile devices cannot write multi-track audio to storage fast enough.
:::
