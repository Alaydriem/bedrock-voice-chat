---
title: "Aternos"
description: "Run BVC on Aternos with the standard Bedrock Addon."
source: https://www.bedrockvoicechat.com/wiki/platforms/aternos/
---
Bedrock Voice Chat works on Aternos as a normal mcaddon. Install the standard [Bedrock Addon](/wiki/server/bedrock-addon/) on your Aternos server and follow the guide there.

`config/default/permissions.json` is set by Aternos to the appropriate permissions. You don't need to modify `config/default/permissions.json` on that platform.

Enable [Beta APIs](/wiki/server/beta-apis/) with the experiment switches in the Aternos control panel.
