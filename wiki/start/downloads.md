## Client app

The Bedrock Voice Chat app lets you talk to other players in Minecraft Bedrock and Java Edition. It uses your microphone and speakers.

| Platform | Where |
|---|---|
| **Windows** | [Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9npxjk015fvt?hl=en-US&gl=US), or [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/Alaydriem/bedrock-voice-chat/releases). |
| **Linux** | `.deb` and AppImage on [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/Alaydriem/bedrock-voice-chat/releases). |
| **macOS** | [TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/JSG7bVqC). One build is for macOS and iOS. |
| **iOS / iPadOS** | [TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/JSG7bVqC). |
| **Android** | [Google Play Beta](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.alaydriem.bvc.client). |

:::tip
Console players on Xbox, PlayStation and Switch use the mobile app on a phone or tablet. The phone or tablet is the microphone and speaker. See [console and mobile](/wiki/platforms/console-and-mobile/).

There is no Bedrock Voice Chat app for consoles. To help fund a console app, support Bedrock Voice Chat on Patreon or as a YouTube member.
:::
