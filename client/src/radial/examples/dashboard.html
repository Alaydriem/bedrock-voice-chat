Why it is shaped this way

Four decisions worth keeping

the ring earns the empty state

In a proximity app you are alone constantly — at connect, while mining, anywhere off the beaten path. That screen is not an edge case, it is somewhere users live, and a live ring says the system is on and listening without asking anyone to interpret it.

and leaves the moment anyone arrives

A circle makes you find a mark, work out whose it is, then find the control. A row gives you a face, a name and a mute button in one glance, which is the only thing that matters when someone is shouting over you.

one axis per surface

The mark owns your mic and flatlines when you are muted. The roster owns incoming and dims when you are deafened. A dot owns the connection. Nothing reports two things, so nothing is ambiguous.

one way in, on both sizes

Pull the list down on a phone, press the dial on desktop. Reconnect lives inside the panel: pulling a list is exploratory and must never take an action on the connection.
