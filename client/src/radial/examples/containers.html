Radial · containers

The row is the unit

A label, at most one sentence of explanation, and one control on the right. Rows stack into a card; a card carries a mono caption. On a narrow container the control drops below the text instead of being crushed against it, and that single change is the whole of what the kit does differently on a phone.

Messages

Four ways to say something is the case

A chip is state in one word, beside the thing it describes. A callout explains, inline, with no action. A banner is page-level and carries an action. A verdict is the headline of a status panel. Collapsing them would mean either a chip that can hold a sentence or a banner that can be ignored, and both are worse than four components.

When data is missing

Loading, failed and empty are three screens

A skeleton says something is coming and roughly what shape it is. A failure needs a reason and a retry. An empty state needs to say how something gets here. One spinner for all three answers none of the questions.

Tables

Search, sort, page and select, as one unit

These four arrive together and they interact: searching resets the page, sorting resets the page, and "select all" means every row matching the current query rather than every row that exists. Wiring them separately is how a table ends up acting on rows the user cannot see.
